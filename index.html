Privacy Policy — Cube Match 3D

Last updated: November 28, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how NPM Tech Solutions ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and handles information when you use the Cube Match 3D mobile game ("the App").

1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information necessary to run the game and improve your experience:

  • Device information: Device model, operating system version, language, country, and screen size — used to optimize gameplay and detect bugs.
  • Gameplay analytics: Levels played, scores, time spent, and feature usage — used to understand how players engage with the game and improve it.
  • Advertising identifiers: Google Advertising ID — used to show personalized ads. You can reset or limit this in your device settings.
  • Crash reports: If the App crashes, we collect anonymous diagnostic data (operating system, device model, time of crash, stack trace) to fix the issue.
  • Purchase records: If you make in-app purchases, Google Play sends us a transaction ID for verification purposes. We never see your payment details.

We do not collect: your name, email address, phone number, exact location, contacts, photos, microphone audio, or any other personal data.

2. How We Use the Information

  • To operate and maintain the App
  • To show ads (including personalized ads where permitted)
  • To process in-app purchases
  • To analyze usage and improve the game
  • To detect and fix crashes and bugs

3. Third-Party Services We Use

We use the following third-party services. Each has its own privacy policy that governs how they handle data:

  • Google AdMob (advertising) — Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Firebase (analytics) — Firebase Privacy Information
  • Google Play Billing (in-app purchases) — Google Privacy Policy
  • Sentry (crash reporting) — Sentry Privacy Policy
  • Expo (app framework) — Expo Privacy Policy

4. Children's Privacy

Cube Match 3D is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the App and provided information, please contact us at the email below — we will delete it promptly.

5. Data Retention

Analytics and crash data are retained for up to 14 months, after which they are anonymized or deleted. Purchase records are retained as required by Google Play and tax regulations.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your region, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold about you. To exercise these rights:

  • Opt out of personalized ads: Reset your Advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Delete game data: Uninstall the App and clear app data via Android Settings → Apps → Cube Match 3D → Storage.
  • Other requests: Contact us at the email below.

7. International Data Transfers

Some of our third-party services (Google, Sentry, Expo) process data outside your country of residence, typically in the United States or European Union. These transfers happen under standard contractual clauses or adequate data protection arrangements.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Material changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
